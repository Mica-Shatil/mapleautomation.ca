Questions, answered

Before we automate.

Do we need to replace our current tools?

No. The first goal is usually to connect the tools you already pay for and remove manual handoffs.

Is this only for AI projects?

No. Many high-value workflows need straightforward rules and integrations, not a language model.

How long does a first workflow take?

It depends on the systems and approvals involved. We start with a scoped discovery conversation and define the smallest useful release.
