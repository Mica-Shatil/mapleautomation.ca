The Workflow
Opportunity Map.

A fixed-scope, fixed-fee first engagement. We map one area of work, put a rough cost on the friction, and give you a ranked, plain-language list of what to automate first. No passwords or system access needed. You keep everything, whether you build with us, with someone else, or in-house.

01

See the real process

A current-state map of one area of work, built from short walkthroughs with the people who do it: who does what, in which tool, and where it waits or breaks.

02

Put a cost on it

A friction and cost estimate using your numbers, with every assumption written down, plus the 2–4 baseline measures to capture before any build.

03

Know what to do first

A ranked list of opportunities (do now, later, or don’t), a recommended first build, a guardrails note on what must stay with a person, and a read-out call.
